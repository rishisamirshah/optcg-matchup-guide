How it wins

The deck is Impel Down Luffy — a tribal go-wide deck whose leader cheats DON from turn 1. Most DON-cheat leaders (Enel, GP) make you wait a few turns; this one re-stands 2 DON frame zero, so every turn you attack with more DON than you "should." You flood cheap, self-replacing 1- and 2-drops, make value attacks every turn, and close with one of two plans.

🗝️ Opponents over-counter early because they fear the end combo — which means your mid-game attacks get through cheap. Win the game through your attacks before the kill turn; the combo is just the finish.

Your two gameplans

Plan A — the OTK ("Luna"): flood a 5-different-named board, then the restand event sets your whole board active for a 10-12 attack alpha in one turn. "7 mana, refresh everything, win the game." Use this vs decks that (1) have no removal for your bodies and (2) don't have a 7k leader.

Plan B — pure RUSH: when the OTK is off, just fill board → swing → refill the slots → kill fast. This is a fundamentals/race deck. This is your plan vs Nami and Enel — see matchups.

Mulligan

Mulligan hard for cheap bodies — multiple 1s and 2s (1c Luffy, Bon Clay, Daz, Ivankov). You want a curve that fills the board, not your bombs. A 1c Luffy is a fine keep — it searches itself into more bodies (Luffy → Luffy → a 2-drop on turn 2 is a dream open).

Take the first 1-2 life hits — you're digging for pieces and you draw off them. Don't fish; just develop.

Turn-by-turn

  • T1 (2 DON): 1c Luffy (search) or a 2-drop. The leader already gives you the extra DON.
  • T2-3: dump cheap bodies — Bon Clay / Daz / Ivankov. Swing with everything; your drops replace themselves so attacking costs you nothing.
  • T4: Croc (on exactly 4 it's "cracked" — bounce+replay a 2-drop, blocker, finds value) or Galdino crack for cards + a stun.
  • T5-6: Buggy → Prisoner is your strongest turn (body + free block + a new name). On 8 DON, double it: Buggy-Prisoner, Buggy-Prisoner.
  • T6+: set up the kill. Boa (unblockable + lock their bomb the turn BEFORE their big turn so you deny 2 attacks, not 1), then the restand for the alpha — OR just keep rushing if they have a 7k leader.

🎯 every turn, get value through attacks. You usually attack 8-12 times across the game before you even need the combo.

Key cards & combos

  • Galdino is the engine, not a body. Almost always crack it immediately (trash → draw 2 + stun a ≤9-cost). It "replaces itself with +4 cards and a negate." Trashing it also keeps your board all-Impel-Down so your restand still turns on. Run 4 (some run extra ST30 copies).
  • Buggy + Prisoner is the backbone — body, free Blocker, and a different name for the restand count. Impact runs 4 Prisoners (not 8) "to fit more 2-drops"; 8 is the stock number.
  • Boa: one on board, not two (it's a brick in multiples). Use the rest/lock the turn before their big swing. You can even Boa their Boa.
  • Restand math: you need 5 DIFFERENT names in play. Watch the trap: two Prisoners on board = you can't restand (same name). Plan your names.
  • Croc / Olive are reactive blockers ("green Borsalino") — play them later/defensively, after the proactive Boa turn.
  • Seat: the deck loves going second — you develop stronger turns and take their curve.

Matchups — the 7k-leader rule

The single most important rule — the OTK is matchup-gated. Impact, verbatim: "This card is giga broken, but it's only your wincon vs decks with no removal AND a non-7k leader. Against Nami and Enel it doesn't work — so the wincon is mostly just playing a rush deck: fill your board, swing, refill your spots."

  • vs Nami / Enel (7k leaders) → RUSH, never OTK. Their leader walls your alpha, so don't durdle for the combo. Flood bodies, Boa-unblockable + Galdino-stun their blockers/defenders, and race them down before they stabilize. Expect them to fight your board / KO your rested bodies — so keep replacing them and keep attacking.
  • vs no-interaction decks (e.g. Yamato, slower midrange) → full OTK. They can't stop the wide board; aggressively counter their early swings (you take a hit + draw off it), build to 5 names, Luna for lethal.
  • vs Lucy → grind + go-wide. Their 5-DON turn is where they remove/wall; they Bari-Bari-Pistol (Sabo) your bodies to break your 5-name count. Keep reloading; take second.
  • vs Roger (counter-event control) → count their counters. Roger + discarded events can burst you; track how many events they can have and don't over-commit into a lethal-back.
  • Mirror: under-explored; freeze wars and value attacks decide it.

Common mistakes

  • Don't OTK into a 7k leader (Nami/Enel). Wrong plan = you durdle and lose the race. Rush instead.
  • Don't sit on Galdino as a 5k body — crack it for the cards + stun unless you're truly stuck for a play.
  • Don't brick yourself out of the restand — 2 same-named bodies (Prisoners) kills the 5-name count.
  • Don't over-commit into the kill turn blind vs counter-event decks (Roger/Yellow) — count their possible counters first.
  • Don't run Brook / "You Can Be My Samurai"-style rest tech — you want to swing your bodies, not tap them. Impact cuts Brook for more 2-drops.
